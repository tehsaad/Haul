Haul privacy policy

Haul has no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry. It doesn't send information about you to the project or to anyone else.

What Haul contacts

- The servers of links you add, to download them. Those servers see your IP address and Haul's name and version, like any web request.
- If you enter a login for a download, it goes only to that download's server. It is kept in memory and forgotten when Haul closes.

The browser extension is optional, and off until you allow it in Settings. When it is on:

- It watches response headers in your browser to notice audio and video. That information stays on your computer and goes only to Haul.
- When a page loads a video stream, Haul fetches the stream's manifest from the server the page is already using, to list its qualities.
- When you click or hover over the list, Haul reads the first bytes of the listed files from their servers.
- When you start a browser download, Haul asks that server about the file, to decide whether it can take over.
- It reads the page's title to name videos. It never reads cookies, passwords or other page text.

How to turn it off

- In the extension's popup, untick "Show Haul on pages" to stop the first three, and "Send browser downloads to Haul" to stop the fourth.
- In Haul, untick Settings > Browser > "Allow the browser extension" to disconnect it completely.

Your downloads and settings are stored only on your computer.
